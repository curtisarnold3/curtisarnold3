# Curtis Arnold

I build small, useful things for the web: a behavior-change app, a few explainers, and some tools I wished existed. Everything below is live and free to poke at.

---

## Live sites

### Books and behavior change

**[REWIRED](https://www.startrewired.com)**
A 66-day accountability app that runs the book's protocol as software. You get one thing to do each day, a streak, and reminders that actually show up.

**[REWIRED: Interactive Edition](https://book.startrewired.com)**
The book *REWIRED: The 21-Day Protocol* as an 18-chapter interactive read, with scroll-driven figures that show the neuroscience instead of just describing it.

**[HOSTS](https://www.starvethehost.com)**
The reader, listening room and storefront for *HOSTS: A Field Guide to the Parasites Eating Your Life, and the Structure That Starves Them*.

### Veterans

**[Claim Basecamp](https://claimbasecamp.com)**
Plain-language help with the VA claims process: a "where do I start" quiz, a claim-paths flowchart, a rating ladder, and benefit pages for every state. Not affiliated with the VA.

**[VA Disability Pay Calculator](https://curtisarnold3.github.io/va-calculator/)**
A free estimator for combined rating and monthly pay. Estimate only; the VA makes the final call.

### Explainers and visual essays

**[Your Mom Loves My AI](https://yourmomlovesmyai.com)**
A visual documentary series on the AI stack, from sand to chips to neoclouds to circular financing. Includes live-updating market tools.

**[The 2030 Agenda, Explained](https://curtisarnold3.github.io/agenda-2030-explained/)**
A source-checked explainer that separates what's documented from what's interpretation.

**[Hearing Where You Are](https://hearing-where-you-are.vercel.app/#tool)**
A walkthrough and hands-on practice tool for working out where a recording was made, by matching the pitch change of an aircraft flyover to public flight tracks.

**[SATCOM Interference Intro](https://curtisarnold3.github.io/satcom-interference-intro/)**
A narrated introduction to spotting and working around a jammer on a satellite link, followed by a live spectrum analyzer scenario you can play with. Fictional and unclassified.

### Tools and learning

**[Learn WCS](https://www.learnwcs.dance)**
A free, scroll-down map of West Coast Swing from beginner to novice: 60+ moves, a progress tracker, and a repertoire view of the moves you own.

**[The Flask](https://www.flasktoplant.com)**
Thirty-six techniques for acclimating tissue culture plants and selling them: the eight-week schedule, the mistakes that cost you a tray, and honest numbers.

**[Mortgage Rate Driver Desk](https://mortgage-rate-desk.vercel.app)**
Splits the 30-year mortgage rate into the 10-year Treasury plus a risk spread, then lets you move each driver to see what it does to your rate and monthly payment. Not financial advice.
